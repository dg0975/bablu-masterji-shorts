# Skit ideas (comedy + science punchline), used top to bottom; log in skits_used.csv

- fridge-jugaad: Golu opens the fridge to cool the kitchen; open fridge heats the room (demo, post first)
- pankha-thanda: Golu turns the fan on in an empty room "to cool it"; a fan cools people (sweat evaporation), not the air
- chashma-bhaap: Papa's glasses fog up over hot chai, Golu blames "bhoot"; condensation on cold glass
- balloon-fridge: Golu's party balloon shrinks in the fridge, he thinks it is "darr gaya"; gas contracts when cold
- chaand-peecha: Golu says the moon follows the car; parallax, far objects seem to move with you
- steel-chammach: Golu leaves a steel spoon in hot dal, Mummy's wooden spoon stays cool; conduction
- cycle-tyre: Golu over-pumps his cycle tyre on a hot afternoon; air expands with heat, pressure rises
- pyaz-aansu: Golu cries cutting onions and claims "emotional scene"; onion gas reacts with eyes' moisture
- echo-ghar: in the empty new flat Golu "talks to his own clone"; echo from bare walls
- namak-barf: Mummy salts the ice for kulfi, Golu calls it "barf ka tadka"; salt lowers the freezing point
- shadow-lamba: Golu thinks he grew taller in the evening; low sun makes long shadows
- thermos-jaadu: Golu: "thermos ko kaise pata garam rakhna hai ya thanda?"; vacuum blocks heat both ways
- mirror-ulta: Golu asks why the mirror flips left-right but not up-down; it flips front-back
- garam-tawa-boondein: water drops dance on Mummy's hot tawa (Mummy demonstrates, Golu watches from afar); Leidenfrost effect
